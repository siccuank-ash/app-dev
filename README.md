# My Favorite Movie

This repository contains one of my favorite movies. It includes the synopsis and my personal thoughts about the story, characters, and overall presentation.

# I Saw the Devil (2010)
## I. Movie: I Saw the Devil
- **Title:** I Saw the Devil
- **Release Year:** 2010
- **Director:** Kim Jee-woon
- **Genre:** Thriller, Crime, Horror
- **Main Cast:** Lee Byung-hun and Choi Min-sik
  
## II. Synopsis
*I Saw the Devil* is a South Korean thriller about Kim Soo-hyun, a secret agent whose fiancée is brutally murdered by a serial killer named Jang Kyung-chul. Determined to get revenge, Soo-hyun tracks down the killer and decides to punish him personally instead of simply turning him over to the police.
As the story progresses, Soo-hyun repeatedly captures and releases the killer, turning their conflict into a disturbing game of revenge. His actions eventually make him question how far he is willing to go and whether his desire for revenge is changing him into the very person he hates.

## III. Thoughts About It
For me, *I Saw the Devil* is one of the most intense and disturbing thriller movies I have watched. What makes it interesting is how it shows that revenge can change a person's character and make them become just as cruel as the person they hate.
I also like how the story keeps me interested because of its unpredictable events and the strong performances of the main actors. Although some scenes are extremely violent, I think the movie has a meaningful message about revenge, anger, and the consequences of letting emotions control your actions.

### My Favorite Things About the Movie
1. The unpredictable storyline
2. The excellent acting performances
3. The dark and intense atmosphere
4. The psychological conflict between the main characters
   
**Movie Rating:** `9.5/10`
